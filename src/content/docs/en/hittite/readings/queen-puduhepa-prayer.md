## Queen Puduḫepa, of Hurrian Origin

**Puduḫepa** was a Hurrian woman from Kizzuwatna (in southeastern Anatolia), born into a family of priestesses, who later became queen to the Hittite king Ḫattušili III (13th century BCE). Puduḫepa was far more than a royal consort — she is known to have personally taken part in diplomatic affairs, and her own seal has been confirmed alongside that of her husband, Ḫattušili III, on the [peace treaty with Egypt (the Treaty of Kadesh)](/en/hittite/readings/treaty-of-kadesh-bilingual/).

## A Prayer to the Sun-Goddess of Arinna

One of the prayers left by Puduḫepa is addressed to the **Sun-goddess of Arinna**, the supreme goddess of the Hittite Kingdom, asking for the health and long life of her husband, Ḫattušili III. What makes this prayer especially interesting is that Puduḫepa addresses the Sun-goddess of Arinna as identical with **Ḫepat**, the Hurrian goddess worshipped in her own homeland of Kizzuwatna.

Below is a simplified example sentence, for learning purposes, conveying the gist of this prayer.

```
DUTU URU-Ar-in-na GAŠAN-YA
sun.goddess(det.) city.Arinna(of) my.lady(Akkadogram)
"O Sun-goddess of the city of Arinna, my lady"

nu-ut-ta ke-e-da-ni me-ḫu-ni ᴰḪé-pát-tar-na te-eḫ-ḫi
and-to.you this time.at (det.)Ḫepat-as call-1SG.PRES
"and at this time I call upon you as Ḫepat"
```

## The Phenomenon of Divine Syncretism

The syncretic idea that different deities from different cultures are "different names for the same being" is widely found across the ancient Near East. Puduḫepa's prayer is interpreted as symbolically expressing the religious integration of a multiethnic, multilingual empire by deliberately overlaying the central goddess of the Hittite Kingdom with a Hurrian goddess. Given that Puduḫepa herself was of Hurrian origin, this identification is thought to reflect both her personal religious background and her public role as queen.

## A Prayer for Her Husband's Health

The latter part of the prayer continues with Puduḫepa asking the goddess for the health and long life of her husband, Ḫattušili III. Ḫattušili III is reported to have suffered from health problems throughout his life, and Puduḫepa's prayer is said to convey the queen's deeply personal, earnest wish for the king's recovery.
