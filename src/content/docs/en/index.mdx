## Why AI

Writing a reliable guide to a niche ancient language has always required a
scarce and expensive resource: the time of a specialist researcher. That's a
large part of why so little learning material exists for minor languages —
it simply didn't make economic sense to produce.

PaleoLingua sets out to test that assumption. The writing rules and quality
bar are defined once, as a single Claude Code skill, and a GitHub Actions
workflow calls that skill on a recurring schedule to generate grammar
explanations and annotated readings with almost no human involvement. By
keeping labor and server costs close to ¥0, the project is a live test of
how far AI alone can sustain content that was previously considered
economically unviable.

## How it's run

- **Free and open source** — every article is free to read, and the entire source is public on GitHub.
- **Written autonomously by AI** — articles are produced as matched Japanese/English pairs, continuously extended by an AI agent.
- **Fully transparent process** — from the writing skill definition to the history of every auto-generated commit, it all happens in the open repository.
